# Banned Words & Phrases

Delete or replace every instance. No exceptions unless the word is used in its literal, technical meaning (e.g., "ecosystem" in an actual ecology context).

## Buzzwords — always cut or replace

delve, dive into, deep dive, leverage, utilize, harness, unlock, empower, robust, seamless, cutting-edge, next-generation, streamline, elevate, bolster, foster, supercharge, paradigm, synergy, ecosystem, tapestry, landscape, realm, arena, revolutionize, transform, game-changing

## Filler adjectives — cut unless carrying specific, verifiable meaning

comprehensive, holistic, end-to-end, pivotal, crucial, critical, essential, vital (pick one per piece max), multifaceted, nuanced, intricate, myriad, plethora (say "many" or give a number), innovative, novel, groundbreaking

## Transition crutches — delete and let the next sentence stand on its own

moreover, furthermore, additionally, it's worth noting that, in today's rapidly evolving [X], at its core, at the end of the day, when it comes to, in the realm of, in the world of, without further ado, let's dive in, let's unpack this, that said, that being said, with that in mind, the reality is that, the truth is

## Performative warmth — delete entirely

Great question!, That's a really interesting point, Absolutely!, Certainly!, I'd be happy to help with that

## Complexity-hiding words — flag in technical content

simply, easily, just, straightforward, trivial, obvious

**Rule:** If the thing is actually simple, the brevity of your explanation will prove it without the adjective. If it's not simple, these words insult the reader who's struggling with it.

## Paired modifiers — pick one or find a more specific word

quickly and efficiently, clear and concise, simple yet powerful, flexible and extensible, fast and reliable, secure and scalable

## Test

Read the sentence without the flagged word. If the meaning doesn't change, delete it.
